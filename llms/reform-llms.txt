# Reform

> Reform is a conversion-focused, no-code form builder for B2B and SaaS teams. It helps you build multi-step, high-converting forms, qualify and enrich leads, reduce abandonment, and sync data with CRMs and marketing tools through deep integrations and real-time analytics.

Reform’s content breaks down into:
- Core product pages explaining features, pricing, templates, and integrations
- Lead Conversion Playbook, a library of tested CRO “plays” for forms and funnels
- Blog, with deep-dive guides on form UX, analytics, privacy/compliance, lead scoring, and integrations
- Legal and security docs, covering data protection, compliance posture, and key policies

Use the sections below as “starting points” when loading context. For broader questions, follow the linked indexes (e.g. `/blog`, `/playbook`, `/play/*`) and selectively pull in additional pages as needed.
